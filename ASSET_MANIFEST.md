## Retained provenance from the earlier prototype

- **Forest wanderer:** Quaternius `Characters_Lis.glb`, formerly loaded from `https://cdn.cinevva.com/assets/packs/quaternius/zombie-apocalypse-kit/Characters_Lis.glb?download=1`. It is documented here for historical provenance but is **not loaded by the current experimental cinematic scene**.

## Environment

The terrain, hills, trees, instanced grass, flower patches, broad-leaf ground cover, lighting, and staging are generated by project code in `index.html` and `grass-system.js`. The current scene loads one warrior GLB and one optional sword prop.

## Runtime dependencies

- Three.js `0.183.0` and its `GLTFLoader` are imported from jsDelivr at runtime.
- The warrior and sword GLBs are loaded from the Cinevva asset CDN.
- This is not an offline/self-contained build. Network availability and CDN CORS policy are runtime requirements.
- Asset failures are logged to the browser console; the page does not display a persistent error overlay.
